Privacy Policy for Month of Doram

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information Collection

We collect the following information to provide and improve our service:

  • Personal Data: If you sign in via Google or Email, we store your email address and profile name to sync your bookmarks and ratings.
  • Usage Data: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized data about app usage (screens viewed, buttons clicked) to improve the app experience.
  • Device Information: We collect basic device info (model, OS version) for crash reporting via Firebase.

2. How We Use Data

  • To maintain your personal bookmarks and watch history.
  • To send push notifications about new episodes (you can opt-out in settings).
  • To show relevant content and improve app performance.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Firebase: For authentication, database, and analytics.
  • Yandex Ads: To display advertisements.
  • Kodik: As a video content provider.

4. Data Deletion

You can request data deletion by contacting us at month.of.doram.support@gmail.com or by deleting your account within the app settings.
