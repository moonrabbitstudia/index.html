Privacy Policy for Month of Doram

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Month of Doram ("Month of Doram", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our Android application.

By using the application, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account and Authentication Information

You may use certain features of Month of Doram without creating a permanent account. If you choose to sign in or create an account, information associated with your authentication method may be processed.

Depending on the authentication method, this may include:

  • Email address.
  • Display name.
  • Profile photo or avatar URL.
  • Firebase Authentication user identifier (UID).
  • Authentication-related information required to maintain your session.

Google Sign-In may provide information such as your email address, display name, and profile image according to the permissions and authentication flow provided by Google.

Email/password authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. Month of Doram does not store your password in its own application database.

Anonymous authentication may be used to provide a temporary authenticated session. Anonymous users do not receive the same community functionality as registered users.

1.2 Favorites, Watch Status and Local Application Data

Month of Doram may store application data locally on your device to provide features such as:

  • Favorites and bookmarks.
  • Watch status and related viewing information.
  • Search history.
  • User ratings and personal comments associated with local content.
  • Notification-related local data.
  • Application preferences and settings.

Local application data may be stored using Android local storage technologies, including SQLite/Room and Android preferences.

1.3 Public Ratings, Reviews and Comments

Registered users may be able to submit ratings, reviews, comments, and replies within the application.

When you submit public community content, the following information may be associated with that content:

  • Rating or score.
  • Review or comment text.
  • Display name.
  • Profile photo/avatar, if available.
  • Dorama reference.
  • Publication timestamp.
  • Likes and dislikes associated with the community content.

Public community content may be visible to other users of the application.

Account deletion and public content: When a registered user deletes their account, personal account information associated with their public reviews and comments is anonymized where applicable. The account identifier and personal avatar information are removed from the review, while the author name may be replaced with a generic label such as "Deleted user". Public community content may therefore remain in anonymized form.

1.4 Likes and Dislikes

When you interact with community reviews using likes or dislikes, the application may associate your Firebase user identifier with those interactions in order to prevent duplicate or inconsistent reactions.

When your account is deleted, your identifier is removed from applicable like and dislike records stored with community reviews.

1.5 Push Notification Information

Month of Doram may use push notification services to deliver notifications such as new episode announcements, application updates, and other relevant application notifications.

Depending on the device and platform, this may involve:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens.
  • Huawei Push Kit tokens on supported Huawei devices.
  • Push notification topic subscriptions.

Where applicable, a push token may be stored in the user's Firestore profile document, for example under users/{uid}/fcmToken.

Push notifications may also use topic-based delivery mechanisms for general application notifications.

1.6 Analytics and Usage Information

Month of Doram may use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the application and to improve functionality, stability, and user experience.

Depending on the enabled analytics configuration, events may include:

  • Application screens viewed.
  • Content opened or interacted with.
  • Search interactions.
  • Favorites and bookmark actions.
  • Ratings and community interactions.
  • Notification interactions.
  • Application settings and preferences.
  • Technical or application events.

Analytics information may be associated with identifiers generated by the analytics service. Analytics data is processed according to Google's applicable privacy policies and configuration of the application.

1.7 Advertising Information

Month of Doram may display advertisements using third-party advertising and mediation services.

Depending on the advertising provider, device configuration, consent settings, and applicable laws, advertising services may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers.
  • IP address.
  • Device type and technical characteristics.
  • Operating system information.
  • Approximate location information where permitted and applicable.
  • Advertising and interaction information.

Current advertising integrations may include Yandex Mobile Ads and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) through the application's advertising configuration and mediation.

Third-party advertising providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements.

1.8 Diagnostic and Crash Information

The application may create local diagnostic information when an application error or crash occurs.

Depending on the diagnostic event, locally generated information may include:

  • Application version.
  • Android version.
  • Device manufacturer and model.
  • Error or stack-trace information.
  • Relevant application performance information.

Diagnostic reports generated by the application's local diagnostic functionality are not automatically uploaded by the application. Where supported, a user may voluntarily choose to share a diagnostic report with the support team.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Month of Doram may be used for the following purposes:

  • To create and maintain user accounts.
  • To authenticate users.
  • To synchronize supported account-related information.
  • To provide favorites, ratings, reviews, and community features.
  • To provide push notifications.
  • To provide and improve application functionality.
  • To understand application usage and performance.
  • To display and measure advertisements.
  • To maintain application security and prevent abuse.
  • To process account deletion requests.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Data Storage

Depending on the type of information, data may be stored locally on your Android device or remotely using third-party cloud services.

3.1 Local Storage

Local application information may be stored in databases and application preferences on your device. This may include favorites, search history, watch-related information, cached data, and application settings.

3.2 Cloud Storage

Account-related information, community content, notification information, and other application data may be stored using Firebase/Google Cloud services.

Access to cloud-stored information is controlled through application authentication and security rules.

4. Third-Party Services

Month of Doram uses or may use third-party services to provide authentication, cloud infrastructure, notifications, analytics, advertising, content metadata, and other application functionality.

  • Firebase Authentication / Google Identity: Authentication and account management.
  • Firebase Firestore: Cloud database services for supported application and community data.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Push notification delivery.
  • Firebase Cloud Functions: Server-side application functionality and notification-related processing where enabled.
  • Firebase Analytics: Application usage and analytics measurement.
  • Yandex Mobile Ads: Advertising services.
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob: Advertising services and advertising mediation.
  • Huawei Push Kit: Push notifications on supported Huawei devices.
  • Kodik: External content and video metadata services used by the application.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Account Deletion

Month of Doram provides an account deletion mechanism within the application.

When a registered account is deleted, the application is designed to perform the following actions where applicable:

  • Delete the Firebase Authentication account.
  • Delete the user's Firestore profile document.
  • Remove stored push notification information associated with the account.
  • Clear relevant local application data from the device.
  • Remove the user's identifier from applicable community reaction records.
  • Anonymize the user's public reviews and comments where applicable.

Anonymized reviews and comments may remain available as community content after account deletion. Personal identifiers such as the account UID and avatar information are removed from applicable review records, and the displayed author name may be replaced with a generic deleted-user label.

Important: Account deletion does not necessarily mean that every piece of content that has been fully anonymized and is no longer associated with your identity will be physically removed from the service.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the application's services, maintain legitimate application functions, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

When an account is deleted, personal account information is removed according to the application's account deletion process.

Public community content may remain in anonymized form when it is no longer associated with the deleted user's identity.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Network communications with supported cloud services use secure transport mechanisms such as HTTPS/TLS where applicable.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

8. Children's Privacy

Month of Doram is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to request access to personal information.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information.
  • The right to request deletion of personal information.
  • The right to object to or restrict certain processing.
  • The right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

These rights may be subject to applicable legal limitations and exceptions.

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us using the email address provided below.

10. Advertising and Consent

Advertising-related processing may depend on your location, applicable privacy laws, device settings, and consent choices.

Where legally required, the application or its advertising providers may request consent before using certain information for personalized advertising or related purposes.

You may also be able to control certain advertising or privacy preferences through Android, Google, Huawei, or the relevant advertising provider's settings.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, third-party services, legal requirements, or our data practices.

When material changes are made, we may update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice through the application or other available communication channels.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, privacy requests, or requests regarding your personal data, please contact:
