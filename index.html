Privacy Policy for Month of Doram

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Month of Doram ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Month of Doram mobile application.

We are committed to handling personal information responsibly and transparently. By using the application, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and Authentication Information

Depending on how you use the application, we may process the following account information:

  • Google Sign-In: email address, display name, profile photo/avatar and the authentication information required to sign in.
  • Email and Password: email address and authentication information managed through Firebase Authentication.
  • Anonymous Authentication: a temporary Firebase user identifier (UID) may be created when anonymous authentication is used.
  • Firebase User ID: authenticated accounts are assigned a unique UID used to associate account-related data.

2.2 User Reviews, Ratings and Comments

If you use the application's community features, we may process:

  • ratings and rating values;
  • review and comment text;
  • replies to comments;
  • display name;
  • profile photo/avatar;
  • dorama reference information;
  • creation or modification timestamps;
  • like and dislike information.

Reviews, ratings and comments may be visible to other users of the application.

Anonymous users may use the application without creating a permanent account, but anonymous users are not permitted to create public reviews or comments.

2.3 Favorites, Watch Status and Local Application Data

The application may store information required to provide personal features such as:

  • favorites and bookmarks;
  • watch status;
  • personal ratings;
  • personal comments associated with saved content;
  • search history;
  • notification-related local settings;
  • application preferences.

Some of this information is stored locally on your device using Android Room/SQLite and application preferences.

2.4 Analytics and Usage Information

Month of Doram uses Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the application and to improve functionality, reliability and user experience.

Depending on the application's configuration and available analytics services, analytics events may include information such as:

  • screens viewed;
  • content opened;
  • episodes interacted with;
  • search activity;
  • favorites and bookmark actions;
  • ratings and community actions;
  • notification interactions;
  • application settings and preferences;
  • technical and application events.

Analytics data is processed by Firebase according to Google's applicable privacy policies and settings.

2.5 Push Notifications

Month of Doram uses push notification services to deliver information such as new episode notifications, application updates and community notifications.

Depending on the device and application version, push notification services may include:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM);
  • Huawei Push Kit on supported Huawei devices.

Push notification tokens may be stored in the user's Firebase Firestore profile, for example under users/{uid}/fcmToken.

The application may also use topic-based notifications, such as the all_users notification topic.

2.6 Advertising Information

The application may display advertisements using third-party advertising services.

Depending on the application version, device and available advertising configuration, advertising services may include:

  • Yandex Mobile Ads;
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob);
  • advertising mediation services.

Advertising providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location and information relating to advertisement delivery and measurement.

Such information is processed by the relevant advertising providers according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

2.7 Diagnostic and Technical Information

Month of Doram may create local diagnostic information when an application error or crash occurs.

Diagnostic information may include:

  • application version;
  • Android version;
  • device manufacturer and model;
  • stack traces;
  • technical performance information.

Local diagnostic reports are not automatically transmitted to our servers by the application's local diagnostic system. Where supported, a user may voluntarily choose to send a diagnostic report to our support team.

3. How We Use Information

We may process information for the following purposes:

  • to provide and maintain the application;
  • to authenticate users and maintain accounts;
  • to synchronize account-related information;
  • to provide favorites, ratings and watch-related features;
  • to provide community reviews and comments;
  • to deliver push notifications;
  • to analyze application usage;
  • to improve application performance and functionality;
  • to display and measure advertisements;
  • to detect and investigate technical problems;
  • to comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Public User-Generated Content

Content that you voluntarily publish through community features may be visible to other users.

Depending on the feature, publicly visible information may include your display name, profile avatar, rating, review or comment text and publication timestamp.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in reviews, comments or other publicly visible content.

5. Third-Party Services

Month of Doram uses third-party services to provide authentication, cloud storage, analytics, notifications, advertising and other functionality.

  • Firebase Authentication / Google: account authentication and identity management.
  • Firebase Firestore / Google: cloud database used for application and user-related data.
  • Firebase Analytics / Google: application usage and analytics measurement.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging / Google: push notification delivery.
  • Firebase Cloud Functions / Google: server-side application functionality and notification processing.
  • Google Sign-In / Credential Manager: authentication using Google accounts.
  • Yandex Mobile Ads: advertisement delivery and measurement.
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob: advertising services and mediation.
  • Huawei Push Kit: push notification delivery on supported Huawei devices.
  • Kodik: external service used by the application for video-related information and episode links.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Storage and Security

Account-related and application data may be stored using Firebase infrastructure and locally on your device.

Local application data may be stored using Android Room/SQLite and application preferences.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. Network communications with our services may use encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Account information is generally retained while your account remains active or for as long as necessary to provide the application's services.

Public reviews and comments may remain available after an account is deleted in anonymized form, where necessary to preserve community content and discussion history.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Month of Doram account and associated personal information.

8.1 In-App Account Deletion

If the account deletion feature is available in your version of the application, you may initiate account deletion directly through the application settings or profile section.

During account deletion, the application may:

  • delete your Firebase Authentication account;
  • delete your user profile from Firestore;
  • remove your push notification token;
  • clear locally stored favorites and related personal data;
  • clear local notification and search-history data;
  • remove your UID from review like and dislike lists;
  • anonymize reviews and comments created by your account by removing account identifiers and replacing the displayed author name with an anonymous/deleted-user label.

Anonymized community reviews and comments may remain in the application after account deletion because they no longer contain the account identifier required to associate them with the deleted account.

8.2 Deletion by Email Request

You may also request account and personal-data deletion by contacting us at:

month.of.doram.support@gmail.com

We may request reasonable information necessary to verify ownership of the account before processing a deletion request.

9. Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • the right to request access to personal information;
  • the right to request correction of inaccurate information;
  • the right to request deletion of personal information;
  • the right to restrict or object to certain processing;
  • other rights provided by applicable privacy legislation.

To exercise applicable rights, contact us using the email address provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

Month of Doram is not intended for children below the minimum age established by applicable law in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, third-party services, data practices or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be published at the public Privacy Policy URL, and the "Last updated" date will be changed when appropriate.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information or account deletion, please contact:

Email: month.of.doram.support@gmail.com
